核心范围 / Core scope

A-01 魔法要改变世界，不只是漂亮 / Magic must change the world, not just look pretty

保留火球点燃可燃物、火遇水熄灭产生蒸汽；火会造成危险，有明确反馈。

Retain fireballs igniting flammable blocks, water extinguishing fire into steam, and readable danger/feedback.

验收 / Acceptance: 正常操作分别验证烧木、遇水、站在火边受伤和恢复。 / Use normal actions to test burning wood, water contact, fire damage and recovery.

A-02 抓鱼 → 烤鱼 → 吃鱼 / Catch → cook → eat

水里要有看得见且可互动的生命；抓鱼、放下、烤熟、拾取、食用与生命/移动效果互相连接。

Water needs visible interactive life; catching, placing, cooking, collecting and eating connect to health/movement effects.

验收 / Acceptance: 从零获得一条鱼，用正常操作走完整条食物循环。 / Obtain a fish from scratch and complete the food loop through normal play.

A-03 昼夜有区别，但动物不消失 / Day and night differ, without deleting animals

太阳白天、月亮夜间；晚上出现怪物，魔法能打怪；鱼与小鸡保持存在，浆果可采集。

Sun by day, moon by night; monsters appear at night and respond to magic; fish/chickens persist and berries are collectable.

验收 / Acceptance: 切换昼夜确认日月、怪物、动物与资源各自的状态；不把白天清怪误写成删掉所有生物。 / Switch day/night and verify celestial, monster, animal and resource states separately.

A-04 保留自由创造与可靠存档 / Keep creative building and dependable saves

保留飞行、挖掘、放置、方块库及保存/导入导出。先复现已有目标，不额外承诺所有Minecraft机制。

Retain flight, mining, placement, inventory and save/import/export. Recreate established goals, not every Minecraft mechanic.

验收 / Acceptance: 搭一个小结构、保存、刷新、导出再导入，验证没有误删旧版存档。 / Build a small structure, save, reload and export/import without deleting baseline saves.

No bedrock-digging feature for its own sake, unlimited Minecraft parity, unrequested cloud multiplayer, store or full asset production.
